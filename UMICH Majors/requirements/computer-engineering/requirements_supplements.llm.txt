Checklist: After Arriving on Campus
1. Obtain an MCard
This is your official University of Michigan identification card. Bring a photo ID to verify who you are. Find more information and campus locations of MCard issuing stations on the
MCard program website
.
2. Update Wolverine Access
It’s important to keep your current personal information (address, phone number, emergency contact) up-to-date. Go to
Wolverine Access
now.
3. Meet with your Academic Advisor
Your
academic advisor
will help you to complete your
Plan of Study
. You will receive your academic advisor at the ECE Program Information Session.
4. Make your final course selection before the first day of class
Check your course information on Wolverine Access. Be sure the courses showing on your class list on Wolverine Access are the classes you are attending.
5. Learn the Bus system
It will make getting around campus a lot easier.
Click here for more information

Grad Cognate Courses
The ECE department requires PhD graduate students to complete cognate courses, which are courses in a discipline or area different from a student’s field of study, but are related or connected with some aspect of this field.
As a general rule, courses that meets one of the following criteria can meet the cognate requirement:
Any course outside EECS department
Any course on the
ECE Course List
that does not have a M or E in the entire row of the course.
Cross-listed courses that are not in your major area
Needs Approval from Major Area Advisor
