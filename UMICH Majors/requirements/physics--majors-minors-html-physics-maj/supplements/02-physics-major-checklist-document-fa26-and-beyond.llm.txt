Elective Courses Requirement:
Four courses (12 credits minimum) of electives chosen from the list below. Note that any course used in the Intermediate and/or Advanced Course requirements cannot also fulfill an elective. Only one course (up to 3 credits) of Independent Study can be used towards the electives.
PHYSICS 340: Waves, Heat, and Light
PHYSICS 360: Honors Physics III
PHYSICS 380/398: Intermediate Topics in Physics, section titled "Introduction to Quantum Computing" (Note: other topics taught as PHYSICS 380 are not automatically included in the major; see an advisor)
CMPLXSYS 335: Networks
Any PHYSICS course numbered at the 400- or 500-level, excluding PHYSICS 501, 517, 518 and 590

Honors Requirements:
Honors requires a cumulative GPA of at least 3.4.
Additional 6 credits of PHYSICS courses numbered 401 and above (not otherwise required, some exclusions apply)
Completion of an Honors senior thesis based on research (PHYSICS 496/497, optional)
Residency requirements:
A minimum of 12 credits toward the major program must be done in residence.
