Honors Requirements:
Honors requires a cumulative GPA of at least 3.4.
Additional 3 credits of PHYSICS courses numbered 401 and above (not otherwise required)
Additional 3 credit course in a cognate
Completion of an Honors senior thesis based on research (PHYSICS 496/497, optional)
Residency requirements:
A minimum of 12 credits toward the major program must be done in residence.
