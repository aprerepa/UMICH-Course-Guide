Honors Requirements
Minimum cumulative GPA of 3.4
Additional 6 credits in PHYSICS courses numbered at the 400 level or above.
Produce a senior thesis (PHYSICS 496/497, optional) under the supervision of a faculty member.
Residency requirements:
A minimum of 15 credits at the 300 level or above must be done in residence.
