Engineering Physics Major
Engineering physics is the study of applying advanced physics concepts and mathematical modeling to solve complex engineering problems across diverse fields such as energy, quantum computing, aerospace, and materials.
If you’re fascinated by how the universe works and want to use that knowledge to build transformative technologies, consider majoring in engineering physics. This interdisciplinary program is ideal for students who enjoy physics, math, and engineering—and want the flexibility to tackle research and design challenges in frontier industries.
Why Engineering Physics at Michigan?
Cross-Disciplinary Flexibility
Students take foundational courses in physics, math, and engineering, then choose a concentration in an area such as quantum science, nuclear engineering, or space systems.
Top Physics & Engineering Programs
Study within one of the best physics departments in the country and alongside world-class faculty in top-ranked engineering disciplines.
Research-Intensive Environment
Undergraduates often contribute to groundbreaking research in areas like quantum materials, plasma science, and particle physics.

Academic Concentrations
The cross-disciplinary nature of this Major allows students to select a concentration from any department.
Aerospace Engineering
The science and practice of flight, split into two branches: Aeronautics—the study and design of aircraft within Earth’s atmosphere; and Astronautics—the study and design of spacecraft that travel beyond Earth’s atmosphere.
Classes:
Solid Mechanics & Aerospace Structures, Aircraft & Spacecraft Propulsion, Control of Aerospace Vehicles
Biomedical Engineering
Applies life sciences and engineering principles to bridge the gap between medical technology and clinical practice.
Classes:
Circuits & Systems, Biomedical Design, Biophysical Chemistry & Thermodynamics
Chemical Engineering
Uses natural sciences, mathematics, and sometimes coding to develop cost-effective and sustainable materials and energy solutions.
Classes:
Material & Energy Balances, Mass & Heat Transfer, Reaction Engineering & Design
Civil Engineering
Designs structures and infrastructure that are safe and foundational to modern society.
Classes:
Sustainable Engineering Principles, Solid & Structural Mechanics, Statics & Dynamics
Climate and Meteorology
Studies the atmosphere and its properties to understand weather, climate, and planetary systems.
Classes:
Earth & Space System Evolution, Atmospheric Thermodynamics, Geophysical Fluid Dynamics
Computer Engineering
Designs and builds computing systems from hardware to software to drive technological innovation.
Classes:
Computer Organization, Signals & Systems, Programming & Elementary Data Structures
Computer Science
Develops new computational methods, architectures, and algorithms to solve complex problems and power modern applications.
Classes:
Data Structures & Algorithms, Computer Architecture, Programming & Elementary Data Structures
Data Science
Extracts actionable insights from complex datasets to solve societal challenges.
Classes:
Applied Regression Analysis, Probability & Statistics, Data Structures & Algorithms
Electrical Engineering
Applies the laws of electricity to develop systems that solve problems across diverse fields.
Classes:
Signals & Systems, Circuits, Electrical Engineering Systems Design
Environmental Engineering
Designs solutions to improve environmental quality and protect public health.
Classes:
Thermodynamics & the Environment, Sustainable Engineering Principles, Hydrology & Floodplain Hydraulics, Environmental Microbiology
Industrial and Operations Engineering
Optimizes complex systems of people, machines, and processes through creative, collaborative problem-solving.
Classes:
Industrial Operations Modeling, Ergonomics, Optimal Methods, Markov Processes
Management Engineering and Finance
Applies systems thinking, finance, and data analytics to optimize decision-making in engineering and business contexts.
Classes:
Economic Decision Making, Optimization and Computational Methods, Introduction to Engineering Analytics, Introduction to Probability, Mathematics of Finance, Corporate Finance, Advanced Laboratory I, Operations Engineering and Analytics, Lean Manufacturing and Services, Decision Analysis and Bounded Rationality
Materials Science and Engineering
Investigates the performance and failure of materials to engineer those with new, advanced properties.
Classes:
Physics of Materials, Solid Mechanics, Kinetics & Transitions in Materials Engineering
Mechanical Engineering
Applies principles of physical mechanics to design and create the technology of the future.
Classes:
Dynamics & Vibrations, Heat Transfer, Circuits, Fluids
Naval Architecture and Marine Engineering
Combines mechanical engineering with large-scale system integration to design marine vessels and ocean structures.
Classes:
Marine Thermodynamics, Vessel/Platform Design, Marine Structures, Marine Hydrodynamics
Nuclear Engineering and Radiological Sciences
Harnesses nuclear energy and radiation to solve problems in energy, health, security, and space.
Classes:
Nuclear Instrumentation Lab, Nuclear Reactor Theory, Thermo/Hydrodynamics in Nuclear Systems
Nuclear Fission
Explores the use of nuclear fission to produce clean energy and support applications in national security, medicine, and industry.
Classes:
Fundamentals of Nuclear Engineering, Elements of Nuclear Engineering I & II, Applied Mathematics for Engineering Physics, Nuclear Reactor Theory, Nuclear Instrumentation Lab, Nuclear Power Reactors, Reactor Safety Analysis, Applications of Radiation
Optical Science and Technology
Applies electromagnetic theory and photonics to design optical systems for communications, imaging, and computing.
Classes:
Electromagnetics, Antennas and Wireless Systems, Principles of Optics, Principles of Photonics, Advanced Laser and Optics Laboratory, Fourier Optics, Optics, Optical Waves in Crystals
Plasma Science and Engineering
Studies ionized gases in applications such as fusion energy, space propulsion, and advanced manufacturing.
Classes:
Introduction to Plasmas and Fusion, Fusion Reactor Technology, Intermediate Plasma Physics I & II, Plasma Engineering, Plasma Generation and Diagnostics Laboratory, Computational Plasma Physics, Space Plasma Physics, Physical Processes in Plasmas
Polymer Science and Engineering
Focuses on the design, synthesis, and analysis of polymers for use in medicine, energy, electronics, and structural materials.
Classes:
Structures of Materials, Design and Applications of Biomaterials, Polymeric Materials, Polymer Physics, Physical Metallurgy, Structural and Chemical Characterization, Kinetics and Transport in Materials Engineering, Ceramic Materials, Rheology of Polymeric Materials
Quantum and Semiconductor Technology
Combines quantum mechanics, nanotechnology, and semiconductor physics to power breakthroughs in computing, communication, and sensing.
Classes:
Semiconductor Devices, Quantum Mechanics I & II, Quantum Nanotechnology, Quantum Information Science, Applied Quantum Mechanics, Power Electronics, Semiconductor Optoelectronic Devices, Solid State Physics
Radiological Health Engineering
Applies radiation science to protect human health and improve medical imaging, safety, and environmental monitoring.
Classes:
Elements of Nuclear Engineering I & II, Nuclear Instrumentation Lab, Radiation Imaging, Radiological Health Engineering Fundamentals, Applications of Radiation, Applied Radiological Measurements, Computation Projects in Radiation Imaging, Medical Physics
Robotics
Integrates mechanical systems, electronics, control theory, and human-centered design to create autonomous and assistive robots.
Classes:
Human-Robot Systems, Robot Sensors and Signals, Robot Construction and Motion, Human-Robot Interaction, Hands-on Robotics, Design and Manufacturing, Probabilistic Methods in Engineering, Manufacturing System Design
Space Sciences and Engineering
Focuses on designing instruments and analyzing data to explore Earth and planetary space environments.
Classes:
Earth & Space System Evolution, Solar-Terrestrial Relations, Radiative Transfer
