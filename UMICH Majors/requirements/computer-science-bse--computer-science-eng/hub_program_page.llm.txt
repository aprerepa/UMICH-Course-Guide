Business and Engineering Dual Degree
Integrated Business and Engineering Dual Degree Program
Students who are interested in Business and Engineering can apply for admission to U-M’s dual degree program in Integrated Business and Engineering. Under this program, students can apply for acceptance into both the Ross Business School and the College of Engineering at once.
Designed to be completed in four years, the dual degree offers integrated coursework in business and engineering and includes action-based learning opportunities and co-curricular activities to elevate your experience.
Engineering students who are interested in studying at the intersection of computer science and business will be able to declare computer science as their major as a part of this process.
For more information and to apply, visit the Business and Engineering Program website
