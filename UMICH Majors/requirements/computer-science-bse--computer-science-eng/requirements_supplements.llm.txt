AEROSP 586 (IOE 586). Air Transportation Systems
Advisory and Enforced Prerequisite: None. (3 credits)
Overview of air transportation systems; course splits into four modules: Airlines, airports, airspace, and emerging topics. Topics include airline schedule development, fleet/crew assignment, airline revenue management; airport capacity and planning, tactical/strategic demand management; air traffic control, deterministic/stochastic air traffic flow management; drone traffic management and air-space integration.
