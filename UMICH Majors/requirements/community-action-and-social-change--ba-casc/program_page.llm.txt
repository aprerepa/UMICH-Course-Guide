Creating the next generation of leaders for social change. The Bachelor of Arts in Community Action and Social Change (CASC) at the School of Social Work prepares students to tackle today's most pressing social challenges through leadership, advocacy, research, and community engagement. Our two-year, full-time, interdisciplinary curriculum combines academic excellence with hands-on learning to give students the skills to create meaningful change in communities, organizations, and public systems. As the nation's top-ranked School of Social Work, students learn from internationally recognized faculty, gaining the experiences and insights needed to make a lasting impact in industry and communities worldwide.
