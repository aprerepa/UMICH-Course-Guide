Sample Course Schedules
The Online MBA Program is self paced and designed for flexibility. On average, most students complete the program within two-and-a-half to three years. However, students are able to accelerate the program to take as few as two years or extend to five years.
You will take core and your choice of elective courses. You have the flexibility to adjust your course load and schedule each semester.
The following courses are provided as examples, course offerings may change.
