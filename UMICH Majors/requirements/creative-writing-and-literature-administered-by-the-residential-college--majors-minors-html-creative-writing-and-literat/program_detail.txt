Creative Writing and Literature (Major)
Creative Writing and Literature Majors write fiction, poetry, and creative nonfiction under the close guidance of faculty mentors and may workshop their writing with other student writers in small writing seminars. Majors also study the art of writing through the study of literature. Majors specialize in fiction/creative nonfiction, poetry, or digital storytelling early in their studies.
