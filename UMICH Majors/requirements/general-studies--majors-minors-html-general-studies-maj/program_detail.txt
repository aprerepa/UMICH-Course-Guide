General Studies (Major)
What do you want to learn? That’s the question at the heart of the Bachelor in General Studies (B.G.S.) degree. B.G.S. is more than a major. It's an interdisciplinary degree that empowers you to design your own academic plan from the start or make the most of your prior courses and experiences.
B.G.S students purposefully combine coursework from different fields to craft a unique, meaningful program of study. The flexible curriculum allows students to integrate diverse learning experiences from study abroad, research, internships, and transfer courses. While B.G.S. has always been a student-driven degree, advisors assist with navigation along the way. The B.G.S. degree cultivates skills in interdisciplinary thinking, creative problem-solving, and self-reflection, making it excellent preparation for graduate and professional schools.
