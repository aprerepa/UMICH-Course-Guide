Archaeology (Sub-Major)
The Archaeology sub-major is intended for students with career interests in archaeology, museum studies, and related fields. Students will explore the human past—including the origins and evolution of culture, inequality, domestication, agriculture, urban life, colonialism and decolonialism—and link this past to the world that we inhabit today. Students will gain skills in archaeological methods such as survey, excavation, and laboratory analysis of material culture to develop a better understanding of how people organized their societies in the past, as well as how such organizations have changed through time. With an emphasis on comparative studies, students will learn from faculty whose field and research interests span Africa, Asia, Europe, the Near East, and the Americas.
