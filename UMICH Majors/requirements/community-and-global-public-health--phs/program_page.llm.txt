Undergraduate Admissions
Pursue a Healthier, More Equitable World
The University of Michigan School of Public Health offers two upper-level undergraduate
                     degree programs, with students completing their junior and senior years at the School
                     of Public Health.
Whether you are a high schooler who is already planning to major in public health
                     at the University of Michigan, a U-M student planning to enroll, or a transfer student
                     from another institution, we have a path to Michigan Public Health for you.
I’m a high school student applying to the University of Michigan
We’re thrilled that you’ve already identified your passion for public health. When
                     you apply to the University of Michigan, you can indicate your interest in majoring
                     in public health as an upperclassman by selecting “School of Public Health” in the
                     preferred admissions of the Common Application. As long as you complete the basic
                     admissions requirements during your first two years as a University of Michigan student,
                     you are guaranteed admission to the public health major. While there are still a few
                     requirements to complete, this route simplifies the admissions process for students
                     who already know they want to pursue public health.
Preferred Admissions
I’m a current student at the University of Michigan - Ann Arbor
If you’re a current student at the University of Michigan - Ann Arbor and have heard
                     about the excellent courses, access to faculty, and student community when you major
                     in public health, you can start planning to apply as a “cross-campus transfer”. Most
                     students apply during their sophomore year, after they have completed the prerequisite
                     courses and have a minimum of 48 transferable credits.
Cross-Campus Transfer Admissions
I’m transferring from another institution
If you’re a student at another four-year institution or community college, the University
                     of Michigan School of Public Health would be thrilled to have you join our pursuit
                     for a healthier, more equitable world. You can apply as an “external transfer” as
                     long as you complete the prerequisite courses and will have approximately
48
transferable credits
before beginning the program.
*International applicants applying as external transfers must complete two consecutive
                        academic years at a U.S. institution before matriculating into the program.
External Transfer Admissions
How we make admissions decisions
Because the world needs public health now more than ever, we’re expanding our bachelor’s
                     degree program. We are seeking a wide variety of applicants who are interested in
                     studying public health. We invite you to think of your application as a way for us
                     to get to know you, what the field of public health means to you, and why the public
                     health major is right for you given your interests, goals, and experiences. We practice
                     holistic admissions, meaning that the admissions committee reviews and weights all
                     of the components of the application equally.
Since our admissions committee does not begin reviewing applications until after the
                     deadline, there is no real advantage to completing your application early. However,
                     we do not encourage students to wait until the last minute, as you might experience
                     technical issues with your application. Submitting prior to the deadline ensures that
                     you will have time to troubleshoot and get in touch with our program staff if you
                     need it.

Prerequisites
All students complete six prerequisite courses as part of their public health degree.
                     While you do not need to finish these courses before applying, we encourage students
                     to take some of them in advance to support a smoother transition into the program.
                     We advise students interested in earning a public health degree to take PUBHLTH 200
                     and STATS 250 as early in their academic career as possible.
Admitted students missing prerequisites will complete them in their first or second
                     semester of the program.
