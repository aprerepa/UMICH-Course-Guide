Mathematics of Finance and Risk Management (Sub-Major)
The program in
Mathematics of Finance and Risk Management
(or
Financial Mathematics
for short) is designed to provide a broad education in the quantitative aspects of risk management and finance. Financial instruments which are engineered today require sophisticated mathematical techniques for their valuation. These techniques come from the fields of probability, statistics and differential equations.
