### Sheet: CSE Course Sorting
CSE Course Sorting Tool 									
Tags are used to provide general categorization of topics covered in our courses. For example, two courses sharing the 'AI' tag will not address Artificial Intellience in the same volume or depth. For full course information, always check with individual instructors (listed in WA) or course sites, as available. Courses that can count for Capstone/MDE requirements may have an overrepresentation of tags given the wide range of topics that can be pursued in course projects.
