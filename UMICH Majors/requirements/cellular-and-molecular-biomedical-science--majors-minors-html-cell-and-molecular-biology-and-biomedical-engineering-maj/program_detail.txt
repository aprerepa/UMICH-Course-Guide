Cellular and Molecular Biomedical Science (Major)
The Cellular and Molecular Biomedical Science (CMBS) program is designed for students enrolled in the College of Literature, Science and the Arts (LSA).  The program is jointly administered by LSA's Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology Department and the College of Engineering's Biomedical Engineering (BME) Department.  A matriculating student will receive the B.S. in CMBS from the College of LSA upon completion of all undergraduate program requirements and is eligible to apply to the M.S. in the BME program in the College of Engineering.
Students may apply to the CMBS program only after completing the major prerequisites.  Admission to the program must be approved by advisors from the Departments of MCDB and BME.
