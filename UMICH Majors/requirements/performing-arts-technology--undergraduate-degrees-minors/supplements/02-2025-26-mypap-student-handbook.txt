ATTENDANCE  &  EXPECTATIONS  
LESSON  ATTENDANCE   Attendance  at  all  lessons  is  required.  Students  should  notify  their  teacher(s)  of  absences  by  contacting  
them
 
directly
 
at
 
least
 
24
 
hours
 
in
 
advance.
 
Lessons
 
are
 
open
 
-
 
which
 
means
 
faculty/students
 
may
 
observe
 
with
 
teacher
 
approval.
  Parents  are  welcome,  and  sometimes  encouraged,  to  attend.  Students  are  expected  to  arrive  at  lessons  
on
 
time.
 
Instructors
 
are
 
required
 
to
 
wait
 
15
 
minutes
 
for
 
a
 
tardy
 
student
 
–
 
after
 
this
 
they
 
may
 
leave.
 
If
 
a
 
student
 
is
 
less
 
than
 
fifteen
 
minutes
 
late,
 
the
 
remaining
 
time
 
will
 
be
 
given,
 
but
 
the
 
lesson
 
will
 
not
 
be
 
extended.
  Parents  are  welcome  to  sit  in  and  watch  lessons,  but  attendance  is  not  required  unless  requested  by  an  
instructor
 
or
 
staff
 
member.
 
Parents
 
should
 
decide
 
whether
 
or
 
not
 
to
 
attend
 
lessons
 
based
 
on
 
how
 
your
 
presence
 
will
 
affect
 
your
 
child.
 
Many
 
students
 
will
 
progress
 
much
 
more
 
quickly
 
when
 
parents
 
do
 
not
 
attend
 
lessons.
 
This
 
is
 
part
 
of
 
the
 
student’s
 
maturation
 
process
 
and
 
is
 
to
 
be
 
encouraged.
 
Should
 
you
 
choose
 
to
 
observe
 
your
 
child's
 
lesson,
 
here
 
are
 
some
 
things
 
to
 
consider:
  ●  Watch  only.  We  ask  that  you  not  break  the  focus  by  asking  questions  or  by  prompting  your  child.  
Remember
 
that
 
your
 
child
 
will
 
learn
 
best
 
if
 
they
 
are
 
the
 
sole
 
focus
 
of
 
the
 
lesson.
 
You
 
may
 
want
 
to
 
write
 
down
 
any
 
questions
 
and
 
save
 
them
 
until
 
the
 
end
 
of
 
the
 
lesson.
 ●  Resist  the  urge  to  give  your  child  an  answer  or  attempt  to  help  them  during  the  lesson.  
Interjections
 
may
 
disrupt
 
your
 
child’s
 
thought
 
process
 
and
 
be
 
more
 
distracting
 
than
 
helpful.
 
The
 
instructor
 
will
 
prompt
 
answers
 
as
 
needed
 
from
 
the
 
student.
 ●  Trust  your  instructor.  All  instructors  are  willing  to  explain  their  methods  and  practices.  If  you  have  
questions,
 
please
 
ask
 
the
 
instructor
 
without
 
your
 
child
 
present.
 
Your
 
goal
 
in
 
watching
 
should
 
be
 
to
 
pick
 
up
 
on
 
ways
 
to
 
help
 
your
 
child
 
at
 
home.
 
You
 
will
 
not
 
be
 
able
 
to
 
learn
 
all
 
the
 
skills
 
your
 
child
 
is
 
learning.
 
The
 
instructor’s
 
focus
 
is
 
to
 
teach
 
your
 
child,
 
and
 
by
 
observing
 
you
 
may
 
be
 
able
 
to
 
help
 
your
 
child
 
remember
 
what
 
the
 
instructor
 
said.

RECITAL  AND  MASTERCLASS  DECORUM  FOR  PARENTS  
AND
 
STUDENTS
 
As  part  of  the  MYPAP  curriculum,  it  is  vital  to  learn  how  to  be  an  outstanding  performer,  and  also  to  be  an  
exemplary
 
audience
 
member.
 
We
 
expect
 
every
 
student
 
to
 
learn
 
proper
 
behavior
 
for
 
both
 
sides
 
of
 
the
 
stage.
 
The
 
following
 
are
 
guidelines
 
for
 
recital
 
performers
 
and
 
audience
 
members:
  For  Performers:  ●  Plan  to  arrive  at  least  30  minutes  before  the  starting  time  of  the  performance  or  masterclass.  ●  Appropriate  performance  attire  is  essential.  Sneakers,  t-shirts,  flip-flops,  jeans,  or  shorts  are  
never
 
acceptable
 
apparel.
 ●  Performers  are  expected  to  acknowledge  the  audience  both  before  and  after  the  performance.  ●  If  performing  with  a  pianist,  it  is  customary  to  acknowledge  the  pianist  to  the  audience  at  the  end  
of
 
a
 
performance.
 
(If
 
you
 
have
 
never
 
taken
 
a
 
bow
 
or
 
acknowledged
 
your
 
pianist
 
before,
 
ask
 
your
 
teacher
 
for
 
a
 
lesson
 
on
 
how
 
to
 
do
 
it
 
properly.)
  
For  Audience  Members:  ●  Attentive  audiences  make  the  best  audiences.  Please  turn  off  all  electronic  devices.  If  you  must  
be
 
on
 
your
 
device(s),
 
please
 
exit
 
the
 
recital/masterclass
 
room
 
and
 
use
 
the
 
lobby
 
space
 
while
 
you
 
wait
 
for
 
the
 
performance
 
/
 
masterclass
 
to
 
conclude.
 
This
 
will
 
be
 
enforced.
 
You
 
may
 
be
 
asked
 
to
 
leave
 
a
 
masterclass
 
setting.
 ●  Audience  members  should  always  arrive  before  the  starting  time  of  the  concert  or  class  (at  least  
10
 
minutes
 
before
 
the
 
listed
 
start
 
time).
 ●  During  a  performance,  concert  hall  doors  are  closed  and  should  not  be  opened  unless  the  
audience
 
is
 
clapping.
 
It
 
is
 
distracting
 
for
 
the
 
performers
 
to
 
have
 
the
 
concert
 
hall
 
door
 
opened
 
while
 
performing.
 ●  Please  remain  in  the  audience  for  the  entire  concert.  Leaving  a  performance  before  it  is  over  is  
distracting
 
for
 
both
 
the
 
performers
 
and
 
your
 
fellow
 
audience
 
members.
 ●  Younger  children  in  the  audience  should  be  kept  as  quiet  as  possible  during  the  performance.  ●  Video  taping  of  MYPAP  recitals/masterclasses  is  permitted  for  personal  use  only.
