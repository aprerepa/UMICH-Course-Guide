### Sheet: Instructions

View the tabs at the bottom to view the Engineering Expertise, Cross-Displicinary, and Profession in Practice courses.

For questions, please email bme-advising@umich.edu or make an appointment with an advisor. 

### Sheet: Engineering Expertise (12 credi
Engineering Expertise (12 credits) Definiation and Requirements:
The Engineering Expertise requirement is designed to provide students with greater depth and rigor in technical areas of engineering (e.g., fluid mechanics, signals and systems, tissue mechanics, and transport) through structured coursework. 
The Engineering Expertise component ensures that students meet the minimum engineering course credit standards set by ABET Accreditation and the College of Engineering. 

*Engineering Expertise must be engineering course work
*Non-BME courses must have Biomedical implications or applications 
*Non-BME courses must have technical rigor, as described in the ABET Outcomes and Program Criteria.
*At least 6 credits must be BME courses
*All courses must be at the 300 level or higher
*Seminar courses (BIOMEDE 500, 550) and independent research (ex: BIOMEDE 490, ENGR 490) do NOT fulfill this requirement
*Up to 2 credits of BME 452 (Biomedical Design II) may count towards this requirement
*All Engineering Expertise courses (beyond the 12 Engineering Expertise required credits) can qualify as Cross Disciplinary credits.
*Courses not specifically listed on the BME Depth Requirement Guide must be pre-approved by the BME department prior to your enrollment (including BME 499/599 courses).

### Sheet: Cross-Disciplinary (6 credits)
Cross-Disciplinary (6 credits) Definiation and Requirements:
- 6 credits of courses in science, engineering, math, or another technical field related to BME (e.g., Entrepreneurship, Bioinformatics, Movement Science, any Med School department)
- Courses must be at the 200 level or higher
- Science courses must be designated as natural science (NS) in the LSA Course Guide.
- Up to 1 credit of seminar may count (e.g., BME 500 Honors Seminar)
- Courses not specifically listed on the BME Depth Requirement Guide must be pre-approved by the BME department prior to your enrollment.
- Up to 2 credits of BME 452 (Biomedical Design II) may count towards this requirement
- All Engineering Expertise courses (beyond the 12 Engineering Expertise required credits) can qualify as Cross Disciplinary credits.

### Sheet: Profession in Practice (3 credi
Profession in Practice (3 credits) Definiation and Requirements:
- 3 credits of a course that is primarily hands-on or experiential learning (e.g., lab, design, practicum, or research)
- Courses must fall under science, engineering, entrepreneurship or computation
- Some BME 499, Multidisciplinary Design Program (up to 3 credits) and some Entrepreneurship courses satisfy this requirement
- Up to 2 credits of BME 452 (Biomedical Design II) may count towards this requirement
- BME 490 may count if it is science or engineering-focused. You must gain pre-approval from the BME Advising Office. 
Students using Directed Research to fulfill this requirement will follow the Directed - - - - -  Research Guidelines. 
UROP can potentially count toward this requirement. Please contact bme-advising@umich.edu for more information.
- Courses not specifically listed on the BME Depth Requirement Guide must be pre-approved by the BME department prior to your enrollment.
