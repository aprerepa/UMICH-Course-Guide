What Will You Do with an LSA Degree?
Frauds and Fantastic Claims in Archaeology.
Physics of the Body and Mind.
Financial Mathematics.
Gender and the Law.
These courses and more are part of a comprehensive LSA curriculum that is thoughtful, practical, and, best of all, overwhelmingly fascinating. The
LSA Course Guide
will help you find available classes searchable by subject, instructor, keyword, and more.
An even broader view of our curriculum is available through the
LSA Course Catalog
– a comprehensive list of all courses currently active and recently offered by the College – searchable by subject, department, or requirement.
Feeling uncertain about what to study? The experts at LSA’s
Newnan Advising Center
can help you develop your academic goals, make informed decisions, and gain independence through access to full-time, peer, and departmental advisors.
