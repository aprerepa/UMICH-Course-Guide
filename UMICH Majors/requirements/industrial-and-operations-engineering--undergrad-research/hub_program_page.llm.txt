What is Industrial and Operations Engineering?
Graduates of the Industrial and Operations Engineering (IOE) major optimize complex systems and processes by collaborating with people, analyzing data and drawing insights from their combined engineering and business mindset. They work across nearly every industry to create innovative, equitable and adaptable solutions to pressing societal challenges.

Minors and concentrations
Human Factors Engineering Minor
The Human Factors Engineering (HFE) minor teaches students design technology that works for the people who use it. Grounded in human-centered engineering, HFE focuses on improving systems, products and environments by making them more usable, efficient, safe and accessible to diverse populations. This is open to undergraduate students outside of IOE who want to gain skills in human factors engineering.
Classes:
IOE 333: Human Factors and Ergonomics, IOE 430: Global Cultural Systems Engineering, IOE 435/535: Quantifying Human Motion Through Wearable Sensors, and more.
Explore the HFE Minor
Financial Engineering Concentration
Gain the skills to thrive at the intersection of engineering and finance. By combining core engineering principles with financial analysis and systems thinking, this concentration prepares you for roles in investment banking, asset management, consulting, corporate finance and more. Available exclusively to U-M IOE undergrads, this concentration gives you an edge in the competitive financial sector.
Classes:
IOE 452: Corporate Finance, IOE 453: Derivative Instruments, IOE 473: Advanced Data Analytic, and more.
