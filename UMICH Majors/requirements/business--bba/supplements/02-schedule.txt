A Schedule That Fits Your Life
Whether you attend the Michigan Ross Executive MBA program in Ann Arbor or Los Angeles, you benefit from the same distinguished faculty and challenging curriculum. You'll follow the same five-term schedule, beginning in August, and meet once a month on campus.
Grow your network and gain new perspectives on business and leadership from your peers, as students from both locations participate in two joint residencies on the Ann Arbor campus during the program.

Stay Connected Wherever You Are
Between once-a-month on-campus residencies, you’ll stay engaged with your studies, faculty, and classmates through distance learning and online collaboration.
The average weekly commitment is 20 to 25 hours per week as you work on deliverables and prepare for the next residency, although this varies by individual and course. Typically, students take three courses at a time. Exams are taken remotely, during a three-day, Friday-through-Sunday schedule.
I have a 4-year-old and a 6-year-old, a husband, and a job, so there are a lot of moving parts. The once-a-month format and different ways the program keeps us connected between residencies are critical. Plus, there are a lot of people in the program who are in a similar life stage. It’s not easy, but we’re in it together striving to be better.
Amanda McVay, MBA
